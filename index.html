Who she is

Favour Ojo is a gospel recording artist, worship leader and songwriter working out of Lagos. Her music is built on the theology of intimacy, the idea that God hears clearest in the quiet.

From the Elshaddai EP through live worship recordings and collaborations, she has landed on a sound that is cathedral-quiet and arena-loud at the same time.
