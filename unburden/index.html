An intimate evening of worship, music and community.

UNBURDEN is an invitation to bring our worries, pressures and burdens before God and be still in His presence.

We’ll worship together, share stories and talk about life and faith. Our hope is that you leave feeling encouraged, reminded that you’re not alone, and connected to people you can keep walking with.

Is it really free?

Yes. Admission is free. Registration is required because seats are limited.

Can I just turn up without registering?

Registering is the only way to guarantee your seat, and it gets you a check-in code for a quick entry. If you do come without registering, there’s a short sign-in at the door.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. Each person needs their own seat, so send them this page and have them register with their own email address.

I registered but can’t find my email.

Check your spam or promotions folder for “Your seat is confirmed — Favour & Faves”. If it isn’t there, fill in the form again with the same email. Your seat stays the same and we’ll send the confirmation again.

Where exactly is it?

Café One, Adeola Odeku, Victoria Island, Lagos. Open in Maps

What if the seats run out?

Registration closes when all seats are taken. Follow @thefavourojo on Instagram for moments from the evening and news of the next one.
